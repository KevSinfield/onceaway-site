# Onceaway 0.5.0 (test2)

A test build for the MacBook trial. It is the first build carrying the
corrected Onceaway brand, and the first that can open the online Help.

## Added

- Links from the app to the online Onceaway Help. **Help → Open Onceaway
  Help** opens the site in your browser, and the Privacy, AI and Assist
  screens link straight to the article that explains them.

## Improved

- The Onceaway mark, the app icon and the menu-bar icon are now drawn from
  one piece of artwork, so they match each other everywhere they appear.
- The tester documents explain what happens when you install a newer build
  over an older one: your history, reviews and settings are kept.

## Fixed

- Installing over an older build no longer risks anything: the database, your
  reviews, your preferences and your AI provider choice are all preserved,
  and this is now checked before every release.

## Known limitations

- This is a preview build, given to invited testers rather than published.
  The installation guide that comes with it covers opening it the first time.
- There are no automatic updates. A new build arrives as a disk image you
  drag over the old one; nothing checks for updates in the background and
  nothing about your installation is sent anywhere.
- AI is optional and off until you add your own API key.
- The Upgrade and Invite screens are previews of ideas. They do nothing.
