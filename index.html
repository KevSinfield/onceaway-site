Why this exists

Every automation tool assumes you already know what to automate. Most people don't.

You just know Friday afternoon went somewhere. Zapier, Shortcuts, macros — they all start with a blank canvas and a question you can't answer: what, exactly, do I keep doing? Onceaway starts by answering that.

How it works

Do it once. Then Onceaway.

01
It observes

The shape of your work — which apps, which files, which sequences — as you go about your day.

02
It notices

The third time you run the same sequence, it's a pattern. Onceaway spots it and counts what it costs you.

03
It suggests

A plain-English description of the repeat, the hours it's taking, and how it could be simplified, delegated or automated.

04
It takes it from here

With your approval, the repeat gets handled — and Onceaway keeps score of the time you got back.

Early access today: observe, notice, suggest. Handling it for you is what we're building next — nothing runs without your say-so.

The Onceaway Principle

If you've shown your computer how to do something once, you shouldn't have to keep doing it yourself.

The first time is work. The second time is a pattern. The third time should already be gone.
